# 🧠 Synapse OS (Scientific Discovery Operating System)

> Accelerating scientific discovery through AI-powered literature analysis, knowledge graphs, and hypothesis generation.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Synapse OS is an AI-powered research platform that helps researchers transform ideas into structured, evidence-backed hypotheses. By combining intelligent literature analysis, interactive knowledge graphs, and multi-agent reasoning, the platform streamlines the early stages of scientific discovery while keeping researchers in complete control of their work.

Whether you're exploring a new research topic, validating existing ideas, or preparing grant proposals, Synapse OS provides a collaborative AI workspace to accelerate discovery.

---

# ✨ Features

## 📚 Literature Management

- Upload research papers (PDF)
- Automatic document parsing
- Bibliography extraction
- Automatic supporting literature discovery
- Paper provenance tracking
- Citation validation
- Paper source badges
- Citation filtering:
  - All Evidence
  - Uploaded Papers Only
  - System-Discovered Papers Only

---

## 🧠 AI Research Assistant

Generate:

- Research summaries
- Concept extraction
- Research gaps
- Evidence-backed hypotheses
- Experimental protocols
- Implications cascade
- Scientific reasoning paths

Each section can be regenerated individually without affecting the rest of the generated research.

---

## 🌐 Interactive Knowledge Graph

Visualize relationships between:

- Concepts
- Papers
- Authors
- Methods
- Research domains

Features include:

- Interactive graph navigation
- Relationship explanations
- Node explanation copy
- Graph analysis copy
- Export graph analysis (.MD, .TXT, .JSON)
- Research Notes panel
- Grant proposal assistance

---

## 🔬 Evidence-Based Hypothesis Generation

Generate hypotheses using:

- Uploaded bibliography
- Optional automatic literature discovery
- AI reasoning
- Knowledge graph analysis

Supporting evidence is transparent and fully traceable.

---

## 📄 Export Suite

Export generated research into:

- PDF
- Markdown (.md)
- TXT
- JSON

## 🔍 Knowledge Graph

The interactive graph allows researchers to:

- Explore relationships between concepts
- Understand supporting evidence
- Discover hidden research connections
- Identify research gaps
- Support proposal writing
- Analyze interdisciplinary links

---

## 📑 Citation Transparency

Synapse OS clearly distinguishes between:

📁 User Uploaded Literature

🌍 Automatically Discovered Literature

Users can choose which evidence sources are used during hypothesis generation.

---

## 🔒 Privacy

- Projects are private by default.
- Uploaded documents remain associated only with the user's workspace.
- Users control whether automatically discovered literature is included during reasoning.

---

## 💡 Vision

Scientific discovery should not begin with weeks of manually reading papers.

Synapse OS helps researchers move from:

**Idea → Literature → Knowledge Graph → Hypothesis → Protocol**

in a structured, transparent, and explainable workflow.

Rather than replacing researchers, Synapse OS augments scientific thinking with AI-assisted reasoning.
